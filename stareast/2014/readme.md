## STAR East 2014 - WebDriver Tutorial and Lightning Keynote

Materials from Alan Richardson's sessions at STAR East in Orlando in May 2014.

More details:

https://www.eviltester.com/conference/stareast2014_conference/

## Content

- [Tutorial: Hands On WebDriver - One Day](tutorial/)
- [Lightning keynote: A Sense of Urgency? or A Sense of Readiness?](keynote/)

The tutorial contains 150 slides and a separate 27-slide exercise-and-answer deck, converted from the conference-specific ODP sources to Markdown. PDF exports were generated with LibreOffice. The keynote was delivered without slides; its folder includes a timestamped transcript derived from automatic captions and links to the recording and the published outline and preparation notes.
