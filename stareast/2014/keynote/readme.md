## Overview

The keynote asks whether you could start testing tomorrow, or now, and explores how to build that readiness through practice and preparation. Alan draws parallels with pulp authors and Max Brand's *Silvertip's Search*.

The talk was delivered without slides. The conference page includes the published outline and explains how Alan developed the talk and used the novel as a prop.

The transcript follows the recording's captions rather than the prepared outline. It has been edited for readability but has not been checked word for word against the audio; its source and uncertain passages are identified in the file.
