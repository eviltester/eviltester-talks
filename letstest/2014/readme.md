## Let's Test 2014 - Automation Abstractions and Skill Acquisition

Materials from the two sessions Alan Richardson presented at Let's Test in May 2014.

More details:

https://www.eviltester.com/conference/letstest2014_conference/

## Content

- [Tutorial: Hands on Exploration of Page Objects and Abstraction Layers with Selenium WebDriver](tutorial/)
- [Workshop: Why You Should Learn Skills That Have No Application in Real Life](workshop/)

Both original conference decks have been converted to Markdown, with their speaker notes kept separately. PDF slide exports were generated from the ODP sources with LibreOffice; the original tutorial diagram handout is also included. PDFs are under each session's `export/` folder.

The tutorial's example code and a later revision of the slides remain in the [Selenium WebDriver Compendium](https://github.com/eviltester/seleniumWebDriverCompendium/tree/main/automationAbstractions).
