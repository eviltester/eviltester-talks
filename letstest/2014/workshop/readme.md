## Let's Test 2014 Workshop - Skill Acquisition and Transfer

Why You Should Learn Skills That Have No Application in Real Life, a workshop by Alan Richardson at Let's Test in May 2014. The source deck also describes it as "Skill Acquisition and Transfer Explored".

## Overview

Participants identify skills they have learned outside their everyday work, practise teaching those skills to others, and examine how motivation, practice, teaching, and feedback relate to software testing. Alan demonstrated juggling and dice stacking; participants shared skills of their own.
