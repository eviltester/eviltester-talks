## History of Hypnosis

It is mandatory to have some padding on the history of hypnosis.

"An Al Oh Gee" Analogy

You will know that hypnosis stems form animal magnetism and the work of Franz anton mesmer in the early 1800s with his hypnotic passes and he had success with his approach through acclaimed case studies. But his explanations and theories were too wild. So other people tried to identify the important parts of his approach.

You will probably know that James Braid is credited with pulling out the notion of fatigue and suggestion as a key element in hypnosis in the 1840s

You may not know that even after braid's death in 1860 he was still viewed as a crackpot and it wasn't until some 'mental health professionals' picked up on it in the 1880s that it gained credibility

Then faded away until the world wars when it was shown to work on stress and in resource strapped battlefield environments.

It gained more respectability when academics and psychologists got hold of it. And they had to standardise it in order to study it, teach it and figure out how it works.

So starting with suggestibility tests "Because they were too intelligent to be suggestible" there was a scale of non-suggestible to highly suggestible, then building 'standardised scripts' that could be read in a monotone (so that no undue influence would occur in the standardised test process) to test people's suggestibility.

That's just what academia does.

Hypnotists can ignore all of that when it suits them, and use psychology when it helps them bolster a suggestion they want to make, use people beliefs 'against them' to effect the changes they want you to make.
