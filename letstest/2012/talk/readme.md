## Testing Hypnotically

Alan Richardson's talk at Let's Test in May 2012. It explores influences from hypnosis on his testing approach, with an emphasis on communication, observation, models, context, feedback, and responsibility.

More details:

https://www.eviltester.com/conference/lestTest2012_conference/

## Conversion

All 47 slides are retained in their original order. The PDF retains the original black background and white text, with the image on slide 6, "As a Young Hypnotist", removed at Alan's request. Literal page-number placeholders from the editable source are omitted from Markdown; the PDF retains its page numbers.

All 74 non-empty speaker-note paragraphs are retained, including the repeated introductory notes attached to several slides. The separate prepared script preserves all 381 source-document paragraphs, including optional material and additional stories. The concise outline preserves all 95 source-map nodes; its detailed narrative notes are represented by the prepared script. These are preparation materials, not transcripts of the delivered talk.

Three native images are extracted from the presentation and reused where they repeat. Slide 9 also has a PNG render to preserve its film-image composition alongside editable text. Only the four images referenced by Markdown are included.

Original presentation, document, and mind-map binaries, duplicate mind-map formats, image editor files, unreferenced artwork, and the image-credit shortcut remain in the source archive. The unrelated Unconventional Influences deck is excluded from this migration at Alan's request.
