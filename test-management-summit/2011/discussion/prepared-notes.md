Title: Automation debate: Open source is the future! Several aspects of automation will be proposed in the debate covering test management; performance testing and functional automation.

## My Open Source credentials:

- Created open source tools: jWinAuto, jCounterString, make window visible, a few others
- Written book on open source tools: Selenium, Java, JUnit, Eclipse (book is not OS, so it is proprietary)
- Use open source tools: Selenium, Java, JUnit, Eclipse, etc.

My position. Not only is open source the future. It is the present. I am astonished that people still use proprietary software and don’t understand why they would do that. So I am hosting this debate to learn reasons why people still use proprietary tools.

## Why people might use proprietary tools:

- The tool is best of breed
- Support Agreement mandated by company
- We own the tool and have no reason to change
- Someone else’s decision
