## Automation Debate: Open Source is the Future!

Alan Richardson's facilitated debate at the UK Test Management Forum on 26 January 2011.

Participants were invited to argue for open-source or proprietary tools, propose other approaches, and vote before and after the discussion. The prompts cover test management, performance testing, test data, functional automation, and manual testing, considering both current experience and the future of automation.

More details:

https://www.eviltester.com/conference/tmf2011_conference/
