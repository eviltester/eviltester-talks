# The rules of the debate

1. An initial vote will be taken. The Debate will commence, chaired and provoked by the chair.
2. Various Topic areas will be provided to build the debate.
3. You will contribute your opinions in an attempt to sway the opposition and floating voters to your side.
4. Contribute your own topic areas during the debate that will make a difference to your position.
5. A second vote will be taken.
6. And lo, the future of Automation will have been decided.
7. New rules will be added as required.
8. Old rules will be broken as required.
