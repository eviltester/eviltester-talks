## Test Management Summit 2014

Materials from Alan Richardson's sessions at the Test Management Summit in London in April 2014.

More details:

https://www.eviltester.com/conference/testmanagementsummit2014_conference/

## Content

- [Tutorial: Introduction to Selenium WebDriver - The Talky Bits](tutorial/)
- [Facilitated session: Automation Abstractions](discussion/)

The tutorial includes a two-slide event introduction and a 76-slide half-day deck. The facilitated session has 22 slides, including prompts for participant discussion. All 100 slides have been converted to Markdown, with three PDF exports in the session `export/` folders. Only images referenced by Markdown are included.
