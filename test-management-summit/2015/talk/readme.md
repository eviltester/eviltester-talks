## Overview

This session explores managing automation, including the challenges faced by managers with different levels of technical experience. It asks what automation is for, how to know whether it works, and how managers can support or obstruct the people building it.

The session was designed around a short introduction followed by participant discussion. The deck includes reserve material to help trigger discussion; the [conference account](https://www.eviltester.com/conference/testmanagementsummit2015_conference/) records that the reserve slides were not needed.

## Markdown Conversion

All 29 slides are retained in their original order. Text slides use editable Markdown, with nested lists and presentation hyperlinks preserved.

The nine reserve mind maps on slides 18-26 are retained as the original embedded pictures, preserving their layout and connectors. They cover definitions of automation, technical skills, management, staff support, assessing results, successful approaches, and recruitment.

Only the 15 image files referenced by the Markdown are included. The original PDF is retained in `export/`.

The separate interview export, long-form preparation-note shortcut, and shortcut to an external project mind map were omitted from this presentation release.
