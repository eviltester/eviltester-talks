## Exploring Open Source Test Tools

Alan Richardson's facilitated session at the Test Management Summit in January 2009. The slide deck uses the title "Open Source & Free Test Tools".

The prompts explore what counts as a test tool, choosing free, open-source, custom, or commercial tools, costs and risks, learning strategies, and effective use. The accompanying article gives examples and exercises, including a worked link-checker selection example.

More details and the expanded session notes:

https://www.eviltester.com/conference/tmsJan2009_conference/
