## How to Manage Technical Testers

Alan Richardson's facilitated discussion at the Test Management Summit in January 2013.

The session asks how a greater emphasis on technical testing changes recruitment, staff development, reporting, risk, and the manager's own skills. The slides invite participants to challenge the premise and bring their own questions and experiences.
