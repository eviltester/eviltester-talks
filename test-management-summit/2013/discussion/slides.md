# The testing world requires more technical testing and testers

- New Technologies
- New Uses for old technologies
- Additional Test Approaches in common use
    - Unit automation, CI
- Technical Projects
    - massively multi-user, performant
- Project Approaches Change
    - Agile, BDD, Lean

Has your environment become more technical?

---

<!-- Slide 7 -->

# It is good that the world requires more technical testing

- More interesting
- More to learn
- We can add more value
- We can cut down on all the dull stuff testing used to do
- We get to work with better skilled people
- We get to work with different attitudes

Do you consider this good or bad?

---

<!-- Slide 8 -->

# Managing technical testers is different

- they don't always do what you say
- they may know more than you about the technology
- they can pursue technology and toys instead of testing
- they leave if they can't be technical
- they are in demand in other places - how do you keep them?

Have you had to change your management style for technical testers?

---

<!-- Slide 9 -->

# Test managers need to remain hands on and technical

- No hiding behind reports and meetings
- No more demanding detailed strategies and plans
- No more letting 'tools' define the process
- Need to keep up with the testers technical knowledge, if not get ahead of them

Is this true for you? If not, what environments do you work in?

---

<!-- Slide 10 -->

# I've changed so that I ...

- focus on staff and skill sets
- do less reporting and fewer meetings
- mentor and train staff more
- have to be hands on
- interview and recruit differently
- massively encourage collaboration
- constantly improve my technical skill and knowledge

How have you had to change? or expect to change?

---

<!-- Slide 11 -->

# I don't see everyone else changing...

- but I don't see the general testing press or book world acknowledging this change
- I see lots of linkedin discussions that suggest many people don't do this, don't want to do this, don't think they should do this, don't seem to have to do this

But I have, and so have most people I know. Is it just us?
