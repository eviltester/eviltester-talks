## Exploratory Testing Techniques

Alan Richardson's presentation at the UK Test Management Summit in January 2010.

The presentation explores techniques and tools, when to use exploratory testing, reporting and tracking its value, and how managers can support, recruit, and coach exploratory testers. Original cartoons accompany the discussion prompts.

More details:

https://www.eviltester.com/conference/tms2010_conference/

Unused image variants, Paint.NET files, original scans, duplicate presentation binaries, and thumbnail caches remain in the source archive. The legacy Flash slide export is omitted.
