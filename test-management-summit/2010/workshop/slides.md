# What do we mean by Exploratory Testing?

- “test design and test execution at the same time” James Bach
- “Exploratory software testing is a style of software testing that emphasizes the personal freedom and responsibility of the individual tester to continually optimize the value of her work by treating test-related learning, test design, test execution, and test result interpretation as mutually supportive activities that run in parallel throughout the project.”
    - Workshop on Heuristic and Exploratory Techniques via Cem Kaner

# Risk Driven Exploratory Testing

- Risk often refers to project risk
- Coverage risk
    - What must work? Higher impact of risk of failure
    - What is less important?
- Targeting risk - what might go wrong risk:
    - Technical Risk – understanding of architecture technology
    - Implementation Risk – understand building tools
    - Usage Risk – scenarios and users
    - Data Risks

---

<!-- Slide 23 -->

# Heuristics &amp; Mnemonics

- Claims, Stress, Risk, Flow, Scenario, Automation
- SFDPOT
    - Structure, Function, Data, Platform, Operations, Time
- CRUSSPIC STMPL
    - Capability, Reliability, Usability, Security, Scalability, Performance, Installability, Compatibility
    - Supportability, Testability, Maintainability, Portability, Localizability
- All above from James Bach's Heuristic Test Strategy Model

# Innovation Leadership Traits

- Combination of creativity &amp; process discipline.
- Accept uncertainty, risks and failures, coupled with an urge to make staff learn from them.
- A passion for their mission and innovation.
- Proactively search for external technologies, and ideas and then experiment with them.
- Courage to stop project not just start them. Able to decide when to Persist vs Pull.
- A talent for building and steering winning teams and attracting and retaining innovators.

from”innovation leaders” by Jean-Philippe Deschamps

---

<!-- Slide 34 -->

# Recruiting Exploratory and Innovative Testers

- Job Spec
    - Be clear about what experience you must have. It must 'rule out' the people who are not suitable.
- Review
    - Review CVs for alternative approaches – Innovative Testers will present themselves as such
- Phone interview
    - Satisfy yourself that they can 'talk' the subjects and have the basic experience for your context.
- F2F Interviews
    - auditions – have them test and explain their thinking.
    - Present candidate with a scenario and see how they explore it, identify risks, any innovative approaches?
