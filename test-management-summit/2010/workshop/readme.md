## Exploratory and Innovative Testing

Alan Richardson's workshop at the UK Test Management Summit in January 2010. The slide deck uses the title "Exploratory Testing & Innovation Workshop".

The workshop combines exploratory-testing exercises and debriefs with discussion of innovation, team culture, risk, heuristics, session-based testing, tool use, recruitment, and coaching.

More details:

https://www.eviltester.com/conference/tms2010_conference/

## Conversion

All 38 slides and 274 non-empty source paragraphs are retained in source order. The 178 embedded speaker-note paragraphs and complete 73-node mind map are converted separately. Timings, network placeholders, tool names, and examples retain their original workshop context. Preparation notes and outline are not transcripts.

The one-slide-per-page PDF was generated with LibreOffice. On slides 10 and 18, the page content is reduced slightly to bring all text inside the page; other generated pages retain their appearance. The original 19-page handout is copied unchanged. It retains the original export's comment indicators and template prompts on section-divider slides.

The workshop contains no raster images to extract. Editable text retains the labels from its shapes. Original presentation and mind-map binaries, the duplicate PowerPoint export, and the notes PDF are omitted after conversion. The distinct handout is retained. Original archive files remain unchanged.

The historical example targets a separately installed Tracks application. The original workshop test environment is not bundled; see the example README for its configuration.
