## London Tester Gathering Workshops 2013

Materials from Alan Richardson's sessions at the London Tester Gathering Workshops in October 2013.

## Content

- [HTTP Proxies and Browser Developer Tools workshop](http-proxies/)
- [Black Ops Testing workshop](black-ops/), with James Lyndsay, Steve Green, and Tony Bruce

The proxy workshop includes 59 event slides, a separate 34-slide tool overview, the original links sheet, and three login-exercise debrief maps converted to Markdown. The two original PDFs are in `http-proxies/export/`.

The Black Ops folder includes all 47 slides recovered from Google Slides, a PDF export, participant-expectation notes, the published session description, and the closing image. Its collaborative preparation document, internal presenter coordination comments, and artwork working files are omitted.
