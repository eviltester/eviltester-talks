## Experiences of Exploratory Testing in Agile, Testing, Dev and QA

Keynote presented by Alan Richardson at Software Development Summit, Helsinki, December 2013.

[Conference details on EvilTester.com](https://www.eviltester.com/conference/sdsummit2013_conference/)

The keynote draws on experience as a developer, tester, test manager, QA lead, and Agile team member. It covers exploratory testing as a learning process, the skills involved, the demands it places on teams and managers, and the distinction between checking and testing.
