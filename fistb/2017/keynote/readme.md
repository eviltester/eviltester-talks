## Overview

Do you automate tactically or strategically? Both approaches are useful and can help our test projects in different ways.

Thinking about automating tactically allows us to move fast, target immediate needs, support testing, and gain benefits very quickly. Automating strategically allows us to gain backing for longer periods of work and justify the cost and ongoing maintenance.

The keynote compares tactical and strategic approaches, explores the dangers and benefits of both, and shows how to combine them to better support testing efforts.
