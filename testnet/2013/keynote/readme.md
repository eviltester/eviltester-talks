## My Quest for Silver Bullets

Keynote presented by Alan Richardson at TestNet, May 2013.

[Conference details on EvilTester.com](https://www.eviltester.com/conference/testnet2013may_conference/)

The keynote reflects on building and using testing tools, the appeal of generic solutions, and the importance of keeping responsibility for testing with the people doing the work. Its central theme is to keep looking for improvements without assuming that one tool or process can solve every problem.
