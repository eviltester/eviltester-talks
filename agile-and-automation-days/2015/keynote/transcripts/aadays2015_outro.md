I gained a lot by reading Stafford Beer's work. He has some good introductions "Diagnosing the System for Organisations" "Designing Freedom" and his very early books "Cybernetics and Management" and "Management Science"

Unfortunately most of these books are out of print, and can be very expensive, but they do turn up cheaply on ebay so keep an eye out. Also Ross Ashby and Norber Weiner are on Archive.org and Guttenberg.
