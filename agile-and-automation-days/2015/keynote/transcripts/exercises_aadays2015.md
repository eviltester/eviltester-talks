## Exercises for "How to Improve your Technical Test Ability"

### Identify Your Limiting Beliefs

"Why?" is a belief targetting question. If there are any parts of this talk that:

* Don't apply to your situation. Ask Why?
* You couldn't use in your environment. Ask Why?
* You don't do. Ask Why?

The answers are you limiting beliefs.

Decide if you want to live with them, or bypass them.

If you want to bypass them. Then make a list of "How" you will do things differently so that you can implement that part of the talk.

### Build Technology Models

Build technology models of the application you test.

Write down all the buzz words associated with your environment. For each buzz word, ask yourself if you 'know' this, or if you are just repeating the word. If you are just repeating it, then add it to your research list.

### Observe, Interrogate, Manipulate

Build a structural model of your application i.e. what components connect to what component. 

Identify 'What' you could observe, interrogate or manipulate e.g. HTML, HTTP messages

Identify 'How' you can observe that component, or the connections between that component and another component. How can you interrogate it or the messages? How can you manipulate

### What tools are in use already?

Make a list of all the tools you use in your environment, by testers, developers, analysts etc.

Do you use the tool?
How could it help you immediately?
Investigate the tool and eexplore its functionality? How could 'you' use that functionality.

Build a list of tools and features to incorporate into your testing.

### What tools can you use?

Look at your 'How' list above and research to find tools that can help you do that.

If you are testing web applications, then start with the tools listed in the talk.

### Start incoporating Observation tools in your testing

Start with 'Observe', and use tools which will help you observe in more detail as you test.

Make sure you research all the things you are observing that you don't understand. This will help you build up your technical knowledge.

### Explore your model for risks

Look at your structural model of your application.

Identify risks in that model e.g. what if the database is offline? What if the message is wrong format?

For the risks, identify:

* How could you trigger the condition that underpins that risk?
* How can you monitor if that risk happens in your environment or in live?

### Interrogate and manipulate

Expand your tool use, to cover interrogation and manipulation and really push your testing to deeper levels.
