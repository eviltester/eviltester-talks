## Introduction

I was invited to the Agile and Automation Days Conference in Krakow, Poland, on the 16th November.

The keynote was called "How to Improve your Technical Test Ability"

I wanted the talk to be 'technical' and show tools that I use, but I also wanted to explain the thought processes behind how you find tools and why we use tools.

I knew that I was going to have to cover some of the objections that 'people aren't technical' or ' they don't have time' and that led into the motif of a Self Help talk.

So I used book covers from some self help books from the office, but I butchered them slightly to have my name and my titles, and my photo - you'll see it in the slides.

The talk works well and gives a good overview of web testing. And both the live and office recordings are good quality. There are more adlibs in the live recording and I think some of the anecdotes are different in the live talk.

It's a good talk, with a lot of important material, I hope you enjoy it and use it as a basis to improve.
