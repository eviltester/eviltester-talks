## National Software Testing Conference 2016 Talk - The Art of Questioning to improve Testing, Agile, and Automating

A talk about using questioning to improve testing, Agile, and automating, drawing on ideas from brief and systemic psychotherapy, the Meta Model, and practical test process improvement.

## Overview

The talk explains how questions reveal and shape models of the world. It compares why questions with how, what, where, when, and who questions, and models testing, Agile, and automating as ways of asking questions and using answers to expand or confirm understanding.
