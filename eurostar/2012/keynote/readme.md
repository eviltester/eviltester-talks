## Unconventional Influences

Alan Richardson's keynote at EuroSTAR in November 2012. The talk explores how experiences and influences outside software testing can change the way we think, communicate, and test.

More details:

<https://www.eviltester.com/conference/eurostar2012-unconventional_conference/>

The paper covers the essence of the talk through an unconventional format; it is a separate work, rather than a slide transcript. The speaker notes, outline, and script are preparation materials, not transcripts of the delivered keynote.

## Conversion

The final 19-slide source and matching PDF from the source `exports/` folder are retained. The older 18-slide copy and 36-page export use an earlier slide order and are omitted. All 98 speaker-note paragraphs, 105 outline paragraphs, 373 script paragraphs, and 101 paper paragraphs are retained in Markdown.

Nine slides also include PNG renders to preserve compositions, diagrams, and image placement alongside editable text. Other images are extracted from the presentation. The paper and slide PDFs retain their original appearance. Only referenced images are included.

The preliminary mind map contains unfinished planning cues and is omitted in favour of the complete prepared outline and script. Duplicate document and presentation exports, image variants, and image editor sources remain in the original archive.
