Unconventional Influences Outline

Aim: tighten up the talk

Main points:

- Software testing does not exist, it is made up, we can continue to make it up
- Trust your influences, you adapt and innovate by bringing in new ideas from outside
- Take responsibility for your testing: approach, communication, models, practice
- Everyone has something unique to add if they talk from experience
- Think critically about your test approach

Change Testing:

- I’ve changed, and adapted, by bringing in more of my influences and life-experiences
    - “I didn’t always believe in a personal test approach, but I do now, because I think we all have to discover the unique value that we can add to projects. So I’ve changed, and adapted. I’ve brought in more of my influences and life experiences.

- I’ve changed the way that I talk about testing, the way that I think about testing, and the way that I do testing. My personal test approach. My unique value.
- the more I do that the more I’ve had to take responsibility
    - “And when you do this, you have to take responsibility for your testing, becuase they are yours, you own them. I’m going to tell you that that gives your more freedom, but you don’t have to believe me. And you shouldn’t believe me. You should be sceptical about everything I say, because while I’m telling you my truth, it might not be THE truth.”

- but one of the reasons I’m grumpy is because people don’t maximise their potential and take responsibility
    - “It might just be because I’m old and grumpy. and I get grumpy with the software testing industry because on the whole it doesn’t encourage personal potential and free thinking, responsibility. It encourages sameness and standardisation. and that leads to lack of resonsibility. And that makes me grumpy.”
