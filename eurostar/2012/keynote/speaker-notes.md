"When he entered the world of testing he left his skepticism, and critical thinking, at the door"

## Slide 5 - “Who madly entered a world of Madness”

Image: <http://drfaustusau.deviantart.com/art/The-Call-of-Cthulhu-Cover-276564815>

Madness.

I had 23 - 24 years of life experience before starting professionally in testing.

For years at University and School I had cultivated a healthy scepticism and healthy mistrust of authority.

And then when I started with testing...

I threw it all away.

Don't do this. Lesson 1. Do not do this.
