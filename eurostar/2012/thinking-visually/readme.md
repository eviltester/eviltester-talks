## Conversion

All 45 slides in the editable source are retained, including seven slides hidden in the original slideshow: 22, 26, and 41-45. These are marked as supplementary material in the Markdown. The matching PDF was generated from that source with hidden slides included.

The separately published 40-page PDF contains 38 visible source slides plus two later promotional pages. It is omitted in favour of the complete source conversion and matching PDF. The 45-page notes export is also omitted after converting all 145 non-empty speaker-note paragraphs to Markdown.

Nine slides include PNG renders to preserve zoomed annotations, spatial layouts, and diagrams alongside editable text. Other images are extracted from the source, including its already-redacted document examples. Only referenced images are included.

The outline preserves 193 source-map nodes; its 17-node internal examples branch, containing preparation and proprietary-source reminders, is omitted. The notes and outline are preparation materials, not a transcript. The complete working documents behind the redacted examples, duplicate mind-map format, image editor files, and cache files remain in the original archive.
