## The Evil Tester's Guide to Technical Testing

Alan Richardson's EuroSTAR webinar on 4 December 2012. It explores technical testing through attitudes, knowledge of technology, models, tools, and examples of how technical understanding changes the risks we investigate.

## Conversion

All 42 slides are retained in order, including the Java examples as fenced code. Two slides also have PNG renders to preserve their arrows and callouts alongside editable text. Other illustrations and screenshots are extracted from the presentation; only referenced images are included.

The PDF is the original slide export. No substantive speaker notes were embedded in the presentation. The separate prepared Word script has been converted to Markdown, preserving all 437 content paragraphs and its illustration, including the section labelled "Unused". The generated contents list and its page numbers are omitted. The concise outline retains all 75 source-map nodes; its detailed narrative notes are represented by the separate script.

The script and outline are preparation material, not a transcript of the recording. Presentation, Word, mind-map, and image editor binaries remain in the original archive.
