## Conversion

The PowerPoint and ODP contain exactly matching text: 184 non-empty slide paragraphs and 29 prepared-note paragraphs across 23 slides. All are retained. The notes are preparation material, not a transcript; the incomplete one-letter note on slide 22 is retained as written.

The Markdown uses the ODP's original cover logo and clear mind-map images, together with the 11 animated GIFs used by the PowerPoint. Two rendered slide compositions preserve the two-column layouts on slides 9 and 18 alongside their editable text. The mind maps link to their annotated Markdown guides.

The clear original PDF, `betterbeta.pdf`, is copied unchanged. The alternative `betterbeta2.pdf` has corrupted mind-map rendering on slides 13 and 20 and is omitted. Six earlier PowerPoint drafts were compared for additional notes; they add no substantive notes beyond a spelling variant documented in the comparison.
