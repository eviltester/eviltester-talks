Extracted from the presentation brief. The submission form fields and biography are omitted.

How quickly can you learn to change, for the better, how you approach software testing? By then end of this session, participants will have learned how I continue to use NLP to do just that, and they will be provided with enough information to start changing their own approach.

Neuro-Linguistic Programming™ (NLP™) provides a diverse set of techniques and models drawn from a wide range of disciplines; including mathematics, linguistics, psychotherapy, systems theory, cybernetics and general semantics. Techniques that have been used to identify, change, and allow people flexibility in their behavior to achieve desired goals effectively and quickly.

This session will provide a short introduction to a subset of NLP techniques and models, which very quickly can be used to improve your own software testing process and increase your awareness for the context within which you test.

Some of the techniques are simple enough that they can be put in practice using the information given solely in this session, and subtle enough that continued usage and study will increase their effectiveness and practical application. I will provide information on where to find more information and how to continue to learn to apply the techniques more thoroughly.

I have personally changed how I view and approach software testing by applying the techniques and models of NLP. During this session, I will provide practical examples of how I use the NLP techniques to improve my own testing.
