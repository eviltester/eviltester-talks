## The Evil Tester's Guide to Evil

Alan Richardson's talk at TestBash in March 2012. The published conference page also uses the title "The Evil Tester's Guide to Eeevil".

The talk explores attitudes, beliefs, humour, responsibility, and building a personal testing approach through knowledge and experience.

More details:

https://www.eviltester.com/conference/testBash1_conference/

## Conversion

All 23 slides are retained in source order. The slide PDF retains its original design and artwork. Slide 2 is an image-only beliefs-and-attitude diagram; its five prepared speaker-note paragraphs are retained separately.

The prepared script preserves all 547 paragraphs from the source document, including optional material and alternative endings. The full outline preserves all 156 source-map nodes. It also retains three narrative paragraphs present in the map but absent from the document export, under Additional Notes from the Mind Map. These are preparation materials, not transcripts of the recording.

The separate one-page PDF is a collapsed mind-map overview, rather than a duplicate slide export. It is retained unchanged alongside the complete Markdown outline.

Twenty-four native images are extracted from the presentation. Slide 5 also has a PNG render to preserve its supervillain-image composition and source credits alongside editable text and links. Only the 25 images referenced by Markdown are included.

Original presentation, document, and mind-map binaries, the duplicate PowerPoint export, unused artwork and contact sheets, and editor/cache files remain in the source archive.
