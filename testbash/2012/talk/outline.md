### 5 whys are belief tools

The 5 why's, now adopted by 6 sigma are belief tools.

They really expose the limits of our creativity - why is the sky blue? because the universe is made of water, because America owns space, because God is blue, pollution from the lead in blue paint, etc. etc.

How? is a process tool that leads to experiments because you can disprove the mechanism.
