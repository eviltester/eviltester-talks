## Words as Symbols - Prepared Alternative

Prepared by Alan Richardson for TestBash 2.0, March 2013. This alternative was not delivered at the conference; Alan gave [Contrast, Analyse, Ridicule](../talk/) instead.

[Conference account on EvilTester.com](https://www.eviltester.com/conference/testBash2_conference/)

Rather than settling on a fixed definition of testing, this talk proposes developing a personal vocabulary of words used as symbols. It considers how those words change as people learn and how they can express current beliefs and responsibility for testing.
