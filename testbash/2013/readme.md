## TestBash 2.0 2013 - 99-Second Talks

Alan Richardson's 99-second talk material for TestBash 2.0, Brighton, 22 March 2013.

- [Delivered talk: Contrast, Analyse, Ridicule](talk/)
- [Prepared alternative: Words as Symbols](alternative-talk/) - not delivered at the conference
- [Conference account on EvilTester.com](https://www.eviltester.com/conference/testBash2_conference/)

Both folders contain a Markdown outline converted from the original FreeMind map and the corresponding published PNG. The outlines preserve the source wording and hierarchy; they are preparation notes rather than transcripts.

The recording titles confirm which map belongs to each version. The home-recorded version of the delivered talk is titled "TestBash 2.0 99 Second Talk Push Your Testing Concepts To Their Limit". The alternative recording is titled "99 Second Talk: Words As Symbols".

No slide deck or PDF was present in the source folder. The original map files remain in the source archive.
