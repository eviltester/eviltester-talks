## Contrast, Analyse, Ridicule

Alan Richardson's 99-second talk delivered at TestBash 2.0, Brighton, 22 March 2013. The recorded home version is titled "Push Your Testing Concepts To Their Limit".

[Conference account on EvilTester.com](https://www.eviltester.com/conference/testBash2_conference/)

The talk encourages testers to examine their vocabulary by contrasting terms with their use in other disciplines, analysing their meaning, and testing whether they stand up to ridicule. It uses the idea of a school-play script to explore interpretation, coordination, and individual performance.
