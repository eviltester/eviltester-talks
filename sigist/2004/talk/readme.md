## NLP for Testers - A Brief Introduction

Alan Richardson. SIGIST, June 2004.

- [Slides - Markdown, 9 slides](slides.md)
- [Slides - PDF, 9 pages](export/slides.pdf)
- [Prepared paper](../paper/)
- [Public talk abstract](../supporting/abstract.md)
- [Conference page](https://www.eviltester.com/conference/sigist2004_conference/)

The talk applies questioning patterns to ambiguity, using an example requirement about processing a daily trade file. It covers generalization, deletion and distortion, then introduces the roots of NLP and further learning.

### About the conversion

The Markdown preserves all 30 non-empty PowerPoint text paragraphs and adds readable text from the embedded diagrams: the Meta-Model hierarchy, requirement annotations, pattern/example tables and NLP-roots diagram. Six rendered slide compositions retain the original diagrams and their layout; the cover uses the native Compendium Developments logo. Only these seven referenced images are included.

The PowerPoint has no embedded speaker notes. Its companion Word document supplies the prepared paper, not a recording transcript. The nine-page PDF was generated from the source PowerPoint with LibreOffice. No retired short links were found in its text or annotations.
