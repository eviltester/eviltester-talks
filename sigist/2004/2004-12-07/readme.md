# SIGIST — 7 December 2004 — NLP for Testers

Alan Richardson's 90-minute NLP for Testers tutorial at BCS SIGIST on 7 December 2004. The date is explicitly recorded on the [NLP for Testers resource page](https://www.eviltester.com/nlp-for-testers/#the-presentation-slides) and in the original recording filenames.

- [Associated exercise notes and preparation maps](supporting/)
- [Talk recording transcript](transcripts/)

This is a separate session from the [June 2004 SIGIST talk](../talk/). The 37-slide tutorial covers NLP models, states, modelling, ambiguity and Meta-Model questioning, with exercises and teaching notes. Supporting development material may postdate the event and is labelled accordingly.

PDF exports are under `tutorial/export/`. Original editor files, recordings, reviewer correspondence and duplicate exports remain in the source archive. Only images referenced by the Markdown are included.
