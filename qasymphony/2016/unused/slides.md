# Process Risk &amp; Consultancy

- As a consultant
    - look at the process a team uses
    - identify Risk
    - extrapolate to think what impact it would have and how it would manifest
    - check that to identify problems quickly
    - identify ways to change process to mitigate/detect
        - Prioritised recommendations
    - client can choose to accept risk now they are aware of it and the impact

# General Risks &amp; Process Risk

“The functionality might not work”

- Some Agile Mitigation
- Automated Unit @Test Assertions
- Agree Acceptance Criteria
- Automate Acceptance Criteria Coverage
- Interactive Acceptance Criteria Review
- Exploratory Testing around Acceptance Criteria

- False confidence from not enough coverage
- Incomplete Acceptance Criteria
    - Story not complete
    - Issues found later
- Not enough time to Automate Acceptance Criteria Coverage
    - Tech Debt Backlog
- Not enough time for exploratory testing
    - Not deep enough
    - Not reviewed for coverage
    - Limited to story not covers system

Risks if we implement this mitigation process poorly
