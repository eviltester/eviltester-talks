# Exercises for "Risk Mitigation Using Exploratory and Technical Testing"

## Analyse your process

* Whatever process you use at work for software development - design, analysis, programming, management, testing, etc.
* Take a step back and analyse it with fresh eyes
* Who is involved?
* What do people do?
* What steps are involved?
* What processes are used?
* What relationships are there between the processes, people and steps?
* What tools are in use?
* What artifacts are created and managed?
* Are there any stated reasons 'why' the process is the way it is?
* Where do the process take place?
* When do they happen? What are the dependencies between the processes, people and steps?
* Compare your model to the reality of the process, what exceptions are there that you haven't modelled?

## Identify issues with your process

* What issues 'always' happen?
* What issues 'sometimes' happen?
* What issues do 'some people' report, but not others?
* What issues do 'people not involved' report and raise?

## Track the issues back to risks

* For each of the issues, identify at least one 'risk' where the 'issue' might result if the risk manifested

## Identify risks around your process

* Using your model of the process, what other risks can you identify?
* What would be the impact if that risk was made manifest?

## Mitigate, Detect or Accept

* Decide if you will mitigate, detect or accept the risks
* Identify mitigating strategies and processes
* Identify detection processes
* Who do you have to communicate the acceptance of the risks to?
* For each of the mitigating strategies and processes - what are the risks that might result from introducing it?
* For each of the detection strategies and processes - what are the risks that might result from introducing it?

## Identify Opportunities

* For each of the risks, can you frame them as opportunities?

## Identify Sources of secondary gain

* For each of the risks, can you identify any secondary gain for any of the parties involved?

## Identify model views/dimensions/perspectives

* For your System Under Test, identify as many different models for it as you can:
    * Different views of the model
    * Different perspectives
    * Different levels
    * etc.

## Identify personal process/knowledge risks

* Given your current approach to how you do your work:
    * What are your personal risks?
    * What issues arise? What risks lead to them?
    * What are your sources of secondary gain?
        * What do they impact?
    * What are the gaps in your knowledge?

## Mitigate, Detect or Accept personal risks

* For each of your personal risks and issues
    * Identify mitigation strategy
    * Are there any signals you can use to detect them?
    * What are you not prepared to accept?
    * What are you prepared to accept?
