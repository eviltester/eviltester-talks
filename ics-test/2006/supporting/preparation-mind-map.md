              > Create a definition of testing.

              > ...

              > It is hard because it won't encompass everythiing that we want about it, everything that we have experienced about it. And part of that is because we are having to generalise as we do it, and we are creating a nominalisation because what we do is not 'testing' it is - bits of analysis, bits of management, communication, bug hunting, investigation, programming, yada yada.

              > So coming up with a definition, isn't going to help you work out what testing is, or what you ultimately think of it, but it might show you what some of your main beliefs about testing are.

              > ..

              > people will argue hard over definitions, when all of them are incomplete in the sense of what I do as a tester or what the testers on my team do.
