## Overview

The talk explores practical automation challenges through three concepts: requisite variety, abstractions, and systems. It draws on hands-on experience of automating applications, finding workarounds, choosing tools, and building flexible abstraction layers.

Historical quotations and comic-book illustrations introduce the themes. The practical examples consider language, BDD tools, Cucumber, and two approaches to coding a TodoMVC scenario.
