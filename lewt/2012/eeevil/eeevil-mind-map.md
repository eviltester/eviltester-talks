# LEWT10 Eeevil

Original FreeMind preparation map. Its internal timestamps record preparation on 9 March 2012; they do not establish the workshop date. Original wording and topic notes are retained.

- **LEWT10 Eeevil**

  > Do you take your 'testing' seriously enough to provoke your beliefs about testing to their very core? How would you tempt yourself off the path of the testing norm? Where would you draw inspiration from? Can you give yourself complete freedom? When you do, where do you draw the lines? Can Alan tell his story to other people without sounding like a lunatic? These questions - and many others - may be answered in the next episode of LEWT.

    - **Born of frustration**

      > How seriously are you going to take testing?

      > I decided a while back that I was going to take it seriously. I was going to own my testing. And I did that because I was frustrated with the way I percieved the testing community.

      > Regardless of the reality, I percieved it as this self-important, pumped up, thou shalt driven black hole of pomposity.

      > That didn't work well. And that was the biggest issue for me.

    - **Danger of seriousness is self-importance**

      > So I built a little cartoon character to help me explore my frustrations.

      > A little court jester.

      > And I latched on to the notion of Eeeevil.

      > Eeeeevil is ridiculous, it is all the bad supervillains, all the bad films you've ever watched, etc.

        - **Eeeeevil**
        - **ridiculous**
        - **provocative therapy**
        - **court jester**
        - **sense of humour mandatory for any change work**
    - **Evil is a tempter**

      > Tempting to do something different.
