# Test Automation Day 2015 Introduction video script

This particular talk was built for the Test Automation Day Conference in Rotterdam held in June 2015

The conference was aimed at managers and practitioners so this talk is higher level than a lot of my automation talks.

I found that I created too much material for this talk so you have the main 45 minute talk but you also have here about 20 minutes or so of extra material.

This was the first talk that I presented which had lessons learned from the history of automation.

My looking in the history of automation started completely by accident when I found an edition of "Automation" by Carl Dreher. And, be honest, how could you not buy it if you saw that cover.

I found some really interesting points in here that I was unaware of - that "Automation" was a 'new' word, and has people credited with creating it as a popular word - i.e. Delmar Harder and John Diebold. Also I didn't' realise that Norbert Weiner didn't like the word automation. And so I bought a whole batch of different books on the history of automation because I needed to check that the quotes and attributions in Dreher's book were correct, and I wanted to go beyond the very narrow quoted history that he had provided so that I could build my own picture of the history of automation.

And I'm still working through that batch of books today.
 
I find looking at 'older' books about computing still adds a lot of value to my learning.

I also found it interesting that many of the problems we face now, are modern instantiations of older problems. And so I weaved in a lot of the older quotes to demonstrate that we might think that our problems are complicated, but from a management perspective many of the lessons have already been learned. From a technical perspective we can pull pointers for general solutions but since we face modern instantiations it means we find new implementations of the solutions but we can find a lot of guidance in the older material.

So I learned a lot preparing this talk. I hope you'll be able to take something from it as well.
