## Experiences with Exploratory Testing

TestIstanbul, May 2013. Keynote by Alan Richardson.

### About the conversion

The selected ODP has 28 slides, including the exploratory-testing survey chart. The matching 28-page PDF export is retained, with retired short-link URLs removed: 28 text occurrences and two baked into the final slide's artwork. No corresponding clickable URI annotations were present. All other PDF text and artwork outside those URL regions remain intact. The shorter 27-page PDF omits the chart and is not duplicated here.

The Markdown retains the slide wording, nested lists, data-template example and editable script table. It includes the five native raster images actually used, plus two rendered compositions preserving the two-column characteristics and responsibility-wordplay arrangement. The table's SVM preview is replaced by its editable Markdown table. Only the seven images referenced by Markdown are included.

The separate `notes.txt` supplies the prepared commentary. These notes cover the opening sections and stop after the lightweight-script example; they are not a transcript. The ODP's embedded notes contain only 24 slide-number placeholders, and the older 24-slide PowerPoint has no speaker notes. Earlier wording is retained in the comparison file.

The ODP, PowerPoint, duplicate PDF and unrelated WebDriver tutorial timing spreadsheet remain in the original archive.
